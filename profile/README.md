## VulWall

Open-source, self-hosted attack surface scanner. VulWall finds your subdomains,
IP addresses and open ports, scans them for vulnerabilities on a schedule, and
explains in plain language what to fix. It runs on your own server, with no
account and no telemetry.

```sh
curl -fsSL https://vulwall.com/install.sh | sh
```
